# Axiom atlas: the essential complexity of human-like intelligence

Started 2026-10-18 (Jesse: "think of essential complexity needed for human like intelligence... explore
axioms across all domains"). This is a living map, not a plan. Each row is a capacity that
human-like intelligence seems to need, what the synthetic brain has for it so far, and candidate
axioms from any domain that could give it. Every candidate is a hypothesis, to be built one stack at
a time with its own check (Rules 1–7).

## Cross-domain axioms that turn up in several capacities

- **Oscillation and resonance** (physics; WAVE-1): binding, attention, frequency lanes, and your
  spiderweb's vibrations.
- **Selection with retention** (evolution, immunology, your protocol): learning, consolidation, and
  growth.
- **Feedback: positive (growth) and negative (the brake)** (chemistry, control): every regulated
  stack.
- **Criticality, the edge of chaos** (physics): where all of it is most adaptable.
- **Temperature as a global dial** (thermodynamics, neuromodulation; T6): mood, arousal, and
  exploration.
- **Compression** (information theory): abstraction, memory footprint, and understanding.

## The largest gaps (a suggested order, one at a time)

1. **Long-term memory by scars** (3): SCAR-1. It is the base for everything that lasts.
2. **Prediction-error learning** (5): the PREDICT/REVISE roots given a real learning signal.
3. **Abstraction and analogy** (8): structure mapping plus invariance. This is the core of how Jesse
   thinks, and the biggest missing piece.
4. **Value and neuromodulation** (12): global dials that make some things matter more.
5. **Consolidation and replay** (13): an offline "sleep" that sorts the scars.
